Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

TTD QuickFill is designed to keep saved information locally on the user's device. We do not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share users' saved personal booking information through our own servers.

Information We Handle

You may choose to save details such as pilgrim names, age, gender, ID proof type and number, mobile number and email address. These are used only to fill booking forms when you ask the tool to.

Local Storage

Saved details are kept in local storage on your device: in your browser's extension storage for the Chrome extension, and in the app's private storage on Android. Uninstalling the extension or app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Data Collection

We do not operate servers that receive your saved booking details. We do not ask you to create an account with us to use the form-filling feature.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your saved booking details with anyone. Details are entered only into the booking pages you open and choose to fill.

Third-Party Services

Some features rely on third parties that operate under their own privacy policies, for example:

  • The official TTD website, where you enter and submit your booking.
  • Google Chrome and the Chrome Web Store for the extension; Android for the app.
  • Services the Android app may use for ads, notifications or app configuration (such as Google AdMob, Firebase and OneSignal), which may process device identifiers or usage data.
  • The payment provider used for the Android app purchase.

Browser and Android permissions requested by TTD QuickFill are used only for the app's stated features. Please review those providers' policies for details on how they handle data.

Security

We take reasonable steps to design the tool so your data stays on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device locked and up to date.
